200-Word Version (Detailed Profile)

Ryan Laird is a senior business development and commercial partnerships specialist operating at the intersection of the European space ecosystem, digital infrastructure, and sustainability. Based in Leicester, UK, his professional work centres on advancing Earth observation data applications, environmental metrics, and evidence-led communication frameworks.

An active contributor to public science engagement and professional governance, Ryan holds multiple fellowship and membership qualifications, including Fellow of the Royal Astronomical Society (FRAS), Member of the Institute of Physics (MInstP), Chartered Marketer (MCIM), Member of the Chartered Institute of Public Relations (MCIPR), and Associate of the Association for Project Management (APM). He completed his formal space education at the International Space University in 2013.

Through initiatives within Impact Orbit Collective, Ryan develops strategic positioning, open-source sustainability platforms, and public science programmes like Leicester Space Week. He is regularly invited to speak on space policy, commercial strategy, and scientific literacy.
