# SpaceUp Leicester

An organic, participant-driven unconference celebrating space exploration, science, and community in Leicester.

## Overview

SpaceUp Leicester was an immersive, volunteer-run unconference where attendees—not schedule planners—decided the agenda, talks, and workshops.

Held across 2018 and 2019, the event brought together students, researchers, engineers, artists, and space fans to share their passions and ideas in an open, collaborative format.

## Why I created it

My motivation was to bring the vibrant, democratic ethos of the SpaceUp movement to Leicester, highlighting the city's rich space heritage and active community.

Unconferences break down traditional hierarchies between speakers and audiences, creating a welcoming space for anyone with a passion for space to take the stage.

## What it did

The event's focus included:

- Participant-led schedules: empowering attendees to propose sessions, discussions, and activities on the day.
- Inclusive community building: welcoming diverse perspectives from across science, art, education, and industry.
- Local space celebration: showcasing Leicester's contributions to space science and research.

## My role

I co-organised and led the planning for SpaceUp Leicester (2018–2019), managing event logistics, partner outreach, brand identity, and community engagement.

## Current stage

SpaceUp Leicester successfully completed its scheduled events in November 2019, leaving a strong legacy of community engagement in the city.

## Longer-term ambition

To inspire future grassroots, community-led space events across the region.
