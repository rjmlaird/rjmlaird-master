# Leicester Space Week

Bringing space, discovery and opportunity into the life of the city.

## Overview

Leicester Space Week is an initiative I am developing as an annual, citywide celebration of space, bringing together public engagement, education, research, industry and culture.

The ambition is to create a shared programme that connects activities across Leicester, giving residents, visitors, students and professionals different ways to explore space and its relevance to their lives.

I want the week to be more than a collection of events. The aim is to connect curiosity with opportunity: helping people discover ideas, meet others and find routes into learning, collaboration and participation.

## Why I am developing it

My motivation brings together my interests in space, science communication and community-building.

I want to help make Leicester’s space identity something people can experience and participate in, rather than simply hear about. That means creating opportunities beyond specialist settings and inviting people into the conversation through education, culture, practical activities and shared experiences.

The intention is to bring public and professional audiences into a connected programme without expecting every event to serve everyone. A family discovering space for the first time, a student exploring careers and a business looking for collaborators should each be able to find a meaningful way to take part.

## What I am working towards

The initiative’s developing focus includes:

- Public participation: creating welcoming opportunities to explore space through talks, demonstrations, exhibitions and hands-on activities.
- Education and skills: helping learners discover routes into further study, relevant skills and space-sector careers.
- Research and innovation: providing opportunities to share ideas and connect researchers, businesses and wider audiences.
- Space and sustainability: exploring how space activities relate to environmental challenges, responsible innovation and life on Earth.
- Culture and place: making room for creative and community-led interpretations of space across the city.
- Collaboration: developing a coordinated programme in which participating organisations contribute their own expertise, venues and activities.

The proposed Space Impact Forum would provide a professional strand within the wider week, complementing public-facing activities with discussion and collaboration around space and its wider impact.

These are programme ambitions, rather than a confirmed schedule of events.

## My role

I am shaping the initiative’s concept, strategy, identity and public-facing communications, drawing on my background in astrophysics, science communication, marketing and digital development.

My work includes developing the website and programme framework, preparing planning and partnership materials, and exploring how the week could be organised and funded.

A central part of this work is defining a useful invitation to prospective founding partners: an opportunity to help shape the programme, agree shared priorities and establish a practical approach to delivery.

I want the initiative to develop through collaboration, rather than present organisations with a finished programme and ask them simply to endorse it.

## Current stage

Leicester Space Week remains an ongoing initiative in development.

The immediate focus is on refining a credible first edition, engaging prospective founding partners and establishing what can realistically be delivered with the people, resources and funding available.

The proposed approach is to develop an initial pilot through Impact Orbit Co., while exploring a founding-partner steering group to help shape its direction. These are elements of the developing model, not a claim that a steering group or formal partnership network is already in place.

I am also exploring whether a Community Interest Company could provide an appropriate long-term home. That decision would depend on the initiative’s development, shared commitment and evidence of public benefit.

## Longer-term ambition

I want Leicester Space Week to become a recurring part of the city’s calendar: a welcoming celebration that connects public curiosity with education, research, enterprise and culture.

Success would mean more than attendance during a single week. It would mean relationships that continue afterwards, clearer routes into participation and a programme that becomes more useful through feedback and experience.

The longer-term aim is to build something recognisably rooted in Leicester, shaped with its communities and collaborators, and open to people who may not yet see space as something for them.
