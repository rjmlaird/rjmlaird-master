# Build4Space

Hands-on hackathons and practical curricula designed to turn space concepts into working applications.

## Overview

Build4Space is an initiative focused on hands-on creation, bringing together developers, students, and space enthusiasts to build practical applications using space data, tools, and technologies.

The ambition is to bridge the gap between theoretical space knowledge and tangible technical output, giving participants the opportunity to prototype real solutions for Earth observation, orbital mechanics, and space science challenges.

## Why I am developing it

My motivation stems from a belief that the best way to understand space technology is to build with it. 

While space data and APIs have become increasingly accessible, there remains a need for practical entry points where people can experiment, collaborate, and gain hands-on experience without barriers. Build4Space was created to foster that experimental mindset.

## What I am working towards

The initiative’s focus includes:

- Hands-on prototyping: designing hackathons and coding challenges centered on real-world space datasets and tools.
- Practical curricula: developing educational resources and guides that make technical integration accessible.
- Collaborative problem-solving: bringing multidisciplinary teams together to tackle environmental, orbital, and exploration challenges.
- Open innovation: encouraging open-source contributions and shareable tools for the space community.

## My role

I shape the concept, curriculum design, and technical frameworks, drawing on my background in astrophysics, software development, and space sector partnerships.

My work involves structuring challenges, developing technical resources, and creating environments where participants can rapidly prototype and test ideas.

## Current stage

Build4Space is an ongoing initiative with completed pilot hackathons and evolving educational frameworks.

The focus is on expanding the curriculum and refining hackathon formats for future collaborative events.

## Longer-term ambition

I want Build4Space to become a trusted practical platform for space innovation, helping developers and learners turn curiosity into functional technology.
