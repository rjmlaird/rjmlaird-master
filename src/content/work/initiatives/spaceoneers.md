# Spaceoneers

Telling the stories of the people, projects, and passions shaping Europe's space community.

## Overview

Spaceoneers is a digital media platform dedicated to capturing the human side of the space sector, highlighting innovators, researchers, and entrepreneurs across Europe and beyond.

The ambition is to go beyond technical specifications to share the personal journeys, creative ideas, and collaborative efforts driving modern space exploration and enterprise.

## Why I was involved

My involvement grew from a shared passion for science communication and a desire to give visibility to the diverse individuals making things happen in the European space ecosystem.

Storytelling plays a vital role in connecting the space sector with wider audiences, inspiring the next generation, and humanizing technological achievements.

## What it does

The platform's focus includes:

- Human-centric storytelling: publishing articles, interviews, and features on space professionals and pioneers.
- Ecosystem connectivity: bridging gaps between different national communities within the European space landscape.
- Inspiring engagement: making space culture accessible and engaging for students, enthusiasts, and professionals alike.

## My role

I contributed to editorial strategy, content development, and community outreach, leveraging my background in science communication and commercial partnerships.

My work involved highlighting key developments, coordinating features, and strengthening connections across the European space network.

## Current stage

Spaceoneers stands as a completed digital media platform and archive of European space storytelling.

## Longer-term ambition

To preserve and celebrate the narrative history of Europe's space innovators while inspiring ongoing collaboration.
