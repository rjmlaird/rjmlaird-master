# SpaceUp Live

Virtual livestream broadcasts extending the participatory spirit of the SpaceUp movement to online audiences.

## Overview

SpaceUp Live was a digital broadcasting initiative designed to maintain the collaborative, participant-driven ethos of SpaceUp events in a virtual format.

The ambition was to connect space enthusiasts globally through interactive live sessions, discussions, and collaborative community broadcasts.

## Why I was involved

My motivation came from a dedication to the SpaceUp community and a desire to adapt grassroots space unconferences into accessible online experiences.

## What it does

The initiative's focus includes:

- Virtual unconferencing: hosting online discussions and presentations centered on community interests.
- Global accessibility: bringing together international participants who could not attend physical events.
- Collaborative broadcasting: utilizing tools like YouTube Live and Zoom to foster real-time interaction.

## My role

I coordinated and produced live sessions, managing broadcast logistics, technical integration, and speaker coordination.

## Current stage

SpaceUp Live completed its series of community broadcasts.

## Longer-term ambition

To support the ongoing evolution of accessible, community-driven space communication.
