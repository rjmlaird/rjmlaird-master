# Spaceoneers Live

Interactive livestreams and virtual broadcasts connecting the space community in real time.

## Overview

Spaceoneers Live is a dynamic broadcasting initiative designed to bring space conversations directly to audiences through live discussions, interviews, and community Q&As.

The ambition is to create accessible, real-time forums where space professionals, educators, and enthusiasts can engage in unscripted dialogue about current missions, discoveries, and industry trends.

## Why I was involved

My motivation stemmed from the need for engaging virtual formats that could unite dispersed space communities during times of remote collaboration and digital connection.

Live broadcasting offers a spontaneity and immediacy that static articles cannot match, fostering genuine community interaction.

## What it does

The initiative's focus includes:

- Real-time discussions: hosting live panel talks and interviews with space sector leaders and innovators.
- Audience participation: enabling viewers to ask questions and interact directly with guest speakers.
- Accessible outreach: broadcasting engaging content across digital platforms to widen participation in space dialogue.

## My role

I helped shape and produce live broadcast sessions, coordinating guest speakers, managing technical setups via YouTube Live and Zoom, and facilitating audience engagement.

## Current stage

Spaceoneers Live completed a successful series of broadcast events connecting international audiences.

## Longer-term ambition

To maintain a legacy of engaging virtual media formats that keep space communities connected and informed.
