# Space Integrity Initiative

Promoting ethical communication, transparency, and peaceful space practices across the global space sector.

## Overview

The Space Integrity Initiative is a mission-driven effort dedicated to upholding high ethical standards, clarity, and responsible stewardship across all facets of space exploration and commerce.

The ambition is to champion transparent communication, debris mitigation awareness, and cooperative frameworks that ensure the peaceful and sustainable use of orbital environments.

## Why I am developing it

My motivation stems from the rapid commercialization and congestion of Earth's orbits, coupled with a need for honest, responsible public discourse about space activities.

As humanity's footprint in space expands, maintaining trust, safety, and ethical integrity becomes paramount. This initiative was created to provide a clear voice and framework for responsible stewardship.

## What I am working towards

The initiative’s focus includes:

- Ethical communication: advocating for honesty, accuracy, and clarity in how space achievements and challenges are communicated.
- Orbital stewardship: raising awareness about space debris, sustainability, and responsible satellite operations.
- Transparency and governance: encouraging cooperative frameworks and ethical guidelines across industry and institutional stakeholders.
- Community standards: building a shared charter for organizations committed to peaceful and sustainable space practices.

## My role

I authored the brand pillars, visual identity, and core charter for the initiative, drawing on my professional background in public relations, policy awareness, and astrophysics.

My work involves defining the initiative's messaging, developing governance frameworks, and engaging advocates for space sustainability.

## Current stage

The Space Integrity Initiative is established with its charter, brand identity, and foundational principles in place.

The focus is on expanding awareness and encouraging adoption of its principles across industry networks.

## Longer-term ambition

I want the Space Integrity Initiative to serve as an enduring ethical touchstone for the global space community, safeguarding the future of orbital access and exploration.
