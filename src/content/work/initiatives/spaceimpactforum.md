# Space Impact Forum

Exploring the intersection of space innovation, investment, and sustainability for a changing world.

## Overview

The Space Impact Forum is a three-day professional event designed to examine how space technologies drive economic growth, environmental stewardship, and sustainable innovation on Earth.

The ambition is to bring together industry leaders, investors, researchers, and entrepreneurs to discuss critical challenges, forge partnerships, and highlight actionable solutions enabled by space data and infrastructure.

## Why I am developing it

My motivation combines my commercial background in the space sector with a commitment to sustainable development and ethical innovation.

As the space economy evolves, there is an increasing need for dedicated spaces where commercial opportunity and environmental responsibility are discussed side by side. The Forum was designed to provide that focused environment.

## What I am working towards

The initiative’s focus includes:

- Innovation and investment: highlighting emerging commercial opportunities and investment pathways within the space sector.
- Space and sustainability: exploring how Earth observation and satellite data address climate, environmental, and societal challenges.
- Cross-sector collaboration: connecting space professionals with industries traditionally outside the sector to unlock new applications.
- Strategic dialogue: hosting sessions, keynotes, and discussions centered on responsible space practices and long-term impact.

## My role

I designed the strategic framework, brand identity, and session architecture for the Forum, drawing on my experience across the European space ecosystem and strategic communications.

My work includes defining the core themes, shaping the event structure, and developing partnership frameworks.

## Current stage

The Space Impact Forum has established its foundational branding and strategic framework as a completed event concept and program.

The focus is on nurturing ongoing dialogue and exploring future editions and collaborative spin-offs.

## Longer-term ambition

I want the Space Impact Forum to serve as a premier recurring gathering for leaders committed to maximizing the positive terrestrial impact of space activities.
