## Overview

**Living with Space 4.0** is a short ESA film about the ways satellites and space applications already support everyday life, from navigation and communications to weather and Earth observation.

## The challenge

Most people use space-based services without noticing them. The film needed to connect familiar daily moments with the satellites that make them possible.

## My role

I contributed to the film through Design & Data.
