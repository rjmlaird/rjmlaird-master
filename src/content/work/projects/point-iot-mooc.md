## Overview

Point.MOOC is the online training element of **POINT.IoT**, an EU-funded
programme designed to stimulate innovation at the intersection of the Internet
of Things (IoT) and European satellite navigation (EGNSS, including Galileo).

The course exists because the programme believes everyone should have access
to the tools needed to support Galileo-enabled IoT solutions. It is a
self-directed, free training programme that lets entrepreneurs develop their
ideas at their own pace, whether they are new to the field or already
established in IoT or Galileo-related business.

## Background: the POINT.IoT programme

POINT.IoT was funded under Horizon 2020 and organised by leading companies
including Verhaert, SpaceTec Partners, Cesah and Orange, with support from the
European GNSS Agency (now EUSPA). It combined several strands:

- **POINT.Cheetah:** scouting and selecting new IoT applications that use GNSS
  technologies for a three-month technical and business sprint with a
  multidisciplinary team of mentors.
- **POINT.Kangeroo:** business training tools to support the teams, including
  the POINT.IoT Entrepreneur Playbook and the MOOC.
- **POINT.MOOC:** a Massive Open Online Course teaching the business basics in
  a modern, interactive way.
- **POINT.Partnerships:** connecting teams with industrial stakeholders as
  development partners, suppliers, clients or investors.

The MOOC broadened the reach of the programme, giving anyone with an idea
access to the same core knowledge shared with selected teams.

## The challenge

Entrepreneurs building Galileo-enabled IoT products face two linked problems:

- Many have a strong idea or technology but limited experience designing a
  viable business around it.
- Others understand business but are unsure where to start with satellite
  positioning, low-power GNSS or Galileo integration.

Point.MOOC was designed to address both in one structured programme, so that
participants could move from an initial idea to a more robust product concept
and business case.

## Course structure

Point.MOOC takes learners through two connected parts, presented as 14
on-demand training sessions in the course introduction (the full curriculum
lists 18 lessons including two welcome modules).

### Part 1: Business design

Learners journey through the customer problem, product concept design,
business model creation and launching a product:

1. Welcome to the business design sessions
2. Understanding your customer and the product-market fit
3. Developing your IoT value proposition
4. Translating customer requirements into technical concept design
5. Creating your Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
6. Designing and differentiating your user-centred product
7. Creating your business model
8. Taking your product to market
9. How to fund your development

### Part 2: Technical training

The technical sessions explain positioning in our connected world and go deep
into Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS) and the European system,
Galileo:

10. Welcome to the technical sessions
11. How does positioning work for the Internet of Things?
12. Using low-power GNSS technologies for your IoT solution
13. The advantages of GNSS positioning
14. What differentiates Galileo?
15. The benefits of processing your GNSS data in the cloud
16. Key technologies for IoT trackers
17. Edge computing
18. Security in IoT and edge computing

## Who it is for

- New entrepreneurs exploring an IoT or location-based idea.
- Established IoT or Galileo businesses looking to strengthen their business
  model or technical approach.
- Developers who want a practical introduction to GNSS and Galileo for IoT
  devices.
- Teams preparing to apply to programmes such as POINT.IoT.

## Why it matters

Satellite navigation is becoming a foundational layer of the connected world,
but turning it into successful products requires both technical understanding
and sound business design. By making that training free and on-demand, Point.MOOC
lowered the barrier to entry for European IoT start-ups and helped spread
knowledge of Galileo's advantages for positioning, velocity and timing,
particularly in demanding urban environments.
