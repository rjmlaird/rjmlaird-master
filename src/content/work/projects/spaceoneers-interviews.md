## Overview

**Spaceoneers** is a platform that tells the stories of people working in and around the space sector. This project covers the interviews published through it.

## The challenge

People who build and operate space missions rarely appear in public coverage of them. The interviews aimed to give those people a voice in plain, engaging language.

## My role

I contributed to the interview series through Design & Data.
