## Overview

This project was a **marketing and communications audit** for Climate Action Leicester and Leicestershire, a network working on climate, energy and the built environment across the county.

## The challenge

Local climate organisations often have strong technical content but limited capacity to present it consistently. The audit looked at how the organisation communicates and where small changes could make its work easier to find and understand.

## My role

I carried out the audit and prepared recommendations for the organisation.
