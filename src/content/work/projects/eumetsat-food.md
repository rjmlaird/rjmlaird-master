## Overview

This project was a film for **EUMETSAT**, the European meteorological satellite organisation, about how satellite data supports **food security in Africa**.

## The challenge

Farmers, planners and aid organisations need timely information on rainfall, drought and crop conditions. The film explains how data from weather satellites becomes information that people can act on.

## My role

I contributed to the film through Design & Data.
