## Overview

The **ESA Business Applications** programme helps companies and entrepreneurs use space technology in commercial products and services. This project covered a series of online webinars for its community.

## The challenge

The programme's audience is spread across Europe and ranges from start-ups to established companies. Online sessions made it possible to share opportunities and case studies without requiring travel.

## My role

I supported the organisation and delivery of the webinars, using Microsoft Teams and WebEx.
