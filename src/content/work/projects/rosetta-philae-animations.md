## Overview

These animations supported ESA's communication of the **Rosetta** mission and the **Philae** lander, which made the first controlled landing on a comet.

## The challenge

Rosetta's journey to comet 67P/Churyumov-Gerasimenko involved orbital mechanics, instruments and a landing sequence that are difficult to describe in words alone. Animation allowed those ideas to be shown clearly.

## My role

I contributed to the animations through Design & Data for ESA.

## Related work

This project is part of the wider Rosetta and Philae communication work.
