## Overview

**Making and testing for Mercury** is a short ESA film about the engineering behind the BepiColombo mission. The ESA-JAXA mission must withstand extremely harsh conditions while in orbit around the innermost planet of our Solar System.

The film shows how materials and components are made and tested on the ground so that they can survive those conditions in space.

## The challenge

Mercury combines intense solar heating with a demanding radiation environment. The editorial task was to make the testing process visible and understandable to a general audience.

## My role

I contributed to the film through Design & Data, as part of the wider ESA Material Labs series.
