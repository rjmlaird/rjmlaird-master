## Overview

The **ESA Green Dossier** is a digital publication from the European Space Agency's Business Applications programme. It shows how space-enabled services, from Earth observation to satellite navigation and communications, can support sustainability and climate action.

## The challenge

Space applications are often described in technical terms. The dossier needed to present them through the environmental problems they help to solve, in a form that businesses, investors and policy audiences can scan quickly.

## My role

I contributed to the dossier as part of my work supporting ESA Business Applications.
