## Overview

The **SGAC Find An Asteroid Search Campaign** was a volunteer asteroid-hunting activity run through the Space Generation Advisory Council (SGAC), the global network for students and young professionals in the space sector.

Campaigns of this kind give participants real survey images and ask them to search for moving objects that have not yet been reported. Candidate detections are measured and submitted for review by professional astronomers.

## The challenge

Asteroid discovery depends on careful, repetitive image analysis. The campaign offered volunteers a practical way to contribute to that work while learning how small-body surveys operate.

## My role

I took part as a volunteer through SGAC, searching survey images for candidate moving objects.

## Related work

The same approach was used in the Pan-STARRS asteroid search campaign.
