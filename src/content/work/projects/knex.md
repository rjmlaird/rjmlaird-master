## Overview

This project used **K'nex** building models to support education and outreach around **Bloodhound SSC**, the British land-speed-record project.

Hands-on construction is a simple way to introduce engineering ideas such as structure, speed and aerodynamics to young people.

## My role

I contributed to the project as part of my involvement with Bloodhound SSC.
