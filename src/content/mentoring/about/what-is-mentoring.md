---
title: "What is specialist mentoring?"
draft: true
---

- One-to-one, goal-oriented support for learning, work habits and self-understanding
- Metacognitive emphasis: how you think, plan, focus and recover
- Tutoring = subject content
- Therapy/counselling = mental-health treatment
- Coaching = broader life/work goals, often less study-specific
- Neuro-affirmative, strengths-based stance
