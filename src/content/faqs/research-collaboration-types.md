I welcome collaborations that connect evidence with wider audiences: public engagement and communication strands in funded projects, advisory input, and joint work on space, Earth observation, sustainability and science communication.

My research background is in astrophysics, including work on cometary nuclei. If your project sits outside those areas, ask and I'll be clear about what I can contribute.
