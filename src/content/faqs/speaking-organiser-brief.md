A short brief is enough to start. Useful details:

- Audience size and background
- Date, time and length
- Format (keynote, panel, workshop)
- Venue or online platform
- Access requirements
- What you'd like attendees to leave with
- Whether the session will be recorded or livestreamed

Tell me about any technical set-up you need, such as slide format, microphones or screens, and I'll confirm what I need from you in return.
