Cancellation and rescheduling terms are agreed in writing when you book, so there are no surprises. If something comes up, tell me as early as you can and we'll look for another time that works.

Because sessions are one-to-one and prepared for the individual learner, please raise any concerns about the terms before the first session rather than after.
