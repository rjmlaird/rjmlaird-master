I work with GCSE and A-level students following the UK curriculum, especially those who want to build confidence, understand the physics properly and learn to apply it to unfamiliar questions.

I also work with curious learners who want to explore astronomy and space science beyond school. Parents and carers are welcome to join the initial consultation.
