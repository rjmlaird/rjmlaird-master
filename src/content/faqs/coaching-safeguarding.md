Safeguarding comes first when working with young people. Tuition is one-to-one and online, and for anyone under 18 the booking is arranged with a parent or carer.

Arrangements for each learner, such as whether an adult is nearby or able to see the session, are agreed before the first lesson. If you'd like to know more about safeguarding or any checks in place, ask and I'll answer directly.
