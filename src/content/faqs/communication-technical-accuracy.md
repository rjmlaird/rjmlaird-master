Accuracy comes from working closely with your subject experts and agreeing who signs off what. I come from a physics and astrophysics background (MPhys), work from primary sources where I can, and ask your experts to review technical claims before anything is published.

We agree the review steps and the number of review rounds at the start of a project, so approval doesn't become a surprise at the end.
