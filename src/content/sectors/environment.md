# Environment

The environment is the living and physical world that supports human life: ecosystems, biodiversity, air, water, land and the processes that connect them.

My work in this sector focuses on making environmental change and environmental data easier to understand. I translate scientific research, Earth observation and sustainability issues into clear narratives, visual communication and digital resources.

## Why it matters

Healthy ecosystems support clean air, clean water, food production, flood regulation, carbon storage and wellbeing. Environmental degradation affects all of these systems, with consequences for both nature and people.

Environmental challenges are interconnected. Air pollution affects health and ecosystems; water management affects biodiversity and resilience; biodiversity loss weakens the systems that regulate climate and support livelihoods. Integrated action across climate, biodiversity and pollution is therefore essential. [279]

## My perspective

I approach the environment as a system of relationships rather than a set of separate issues.

Air, water, land, wildlife and human activity are connected. Understanding those connections helps explain why environmental protection is not only about protecting individual species or places, but about maintaining the systems that support life.

My background is in physics, and my work sits at the intersection of environment, climate, space and science communication. I am particularly interested in Earth observation: how satellite and environmental data can make change visible, support monitoring and inform practical action.

## The work I do

### Environmental communication

I help explain environmental issues in ways that are accurate, engaging and relevant to their audience.

This can include explaining ecosystems, pollution, conservation, environmental policy, monitoring data or the relationship between local places and wider environmental systems.

### Making environmental data meaningful

Environmental data can reveal patterns that are difficult to see directly: changing air quality, land use, water conditions, habitat loss or the effects of extreme weather.

I help turn data into narratives and visual explanations that make those patterns understandable, while being clear about uncertainty and limitations.

### Earth observation for environmental understanding

Satellite observations provide a consistent view of environmental change across large areas and over time. They can support monitoring of land, oceans, atmosphere and emergency-response needs.

I am interested in how Earth-observation data can support environmental monitoring, public understanding and practical decision-making.

### Conservation and sustainability

Environmental work is not only about describing problems. It is also about identifying practical responses: protecting habitats, reducing pollution, restoring ecosystems and managing resources sustainably.

I help communicate these responses in ways that are grounded in evidence and realistic about trade-offs.
