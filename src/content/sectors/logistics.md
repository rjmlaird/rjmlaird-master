# Logistics

Logistics is the system that moves goods, materials and information from where they are produced to where they are needed. It connects suppliers, manufacturers, warehouses, transport networks, retailers and customers.

My work in this sector focuses on making logistics systems and their sustainability challenges easier to understand. I translate operational, environmental and data-led material into clear communication and practical digital resources.

## Why it matters

Logistics affects almost every part of modern life. It determines how food reaches shops, how materials reach construction sites, how products reach customers and how emergency supplies reach communities.

It also has a significant environmental footprint. Freight, warehousing and distribution involve energy use, emissions, land use and infrastructure decisions. Improving logistics therefore matters for efficiency, resilience and climate goals.

## My perspective

I approach logistics as a system of flows: goods, vehicles, people, data and decisions.

A delivery is not just a journey from A to B. It is the result of demand, inventory, routing, storage, infrastructure, timing and coordination. Understanding those connections helps explain why small changes can have wide effects.

My background is in physics, and my work sits at the intersection of logistics, sustainability, climate and communication. I am particularly interested in how data and digital tools can make complex operations more visible and support better decisions.

## The work I do

### Explaining supply chains

I help explain how supply chains work and where their risks and opportunities lie.

This can include sourcing, production, warehousing, distribution, last-mile delivery, inventory and the relationships between different parts of a network.

### Communicating sustainability

Logistics decarbonisation involves vehicle technology, route planning, consolidation, modal shift, warehouse energy use and operational efficiency.

I help organisations explain what they are doing, why it matters and what evidence supports their progress, without overstating claims or ignoring trade-offs.

### Making operational data meaningful

Logistics generates data about movements, deliveries, inventory, emissions, delays and capacity. The value comes from turning that data into insight.

I help create clear narratives and visual explanations that connect operational data to practical questions: reliability, cost, resilience, emissions and customer experience.

### Digital tools and visibility

Digital tools can make logistics systems easier to understand and manage. Catalogues, dashboards, maps and data-led resources can help people see what is happening across a network.

My focus is on clarity and usefulness: helping people understand the system rather than adding another layer of complexity.
