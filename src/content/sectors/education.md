# Education

Education is about helping people understand the world and their place in it. It includes formal teaching, informal learning, public engagement, professional development and the digital tools that make knowledge easier to explore.

My work in this sector focuses on making complex subjects clearer and more engaging. I turn scientific, technical and environmental ideas into learning experiences, explanations and resources that support curiosity, understanding and confidence.

## Why it matters

Good education does more than transfer facts. It helps people develop the ability to ask questions, evaluate evidence, understand uncertainty and connect ideas to the world around them.

This matters particularly for science, climate and space. These subjects can feel distant or technical, yet they shape decisions about health, energy, the environment, technology and the future.

Education is also a bridge between research and public understanding. Clear explanations, well-designed resources and accessible digital experiences can help people move from curiosity to genuine understanding.

## My perspective

I approach education as translation with integrity.

The aim is not to simplify an idea until it becomes misleading. It is to identify the core concept, choose an appropriate starting point and build understanding step by step. That means being clear about what is known, what is uncertain and what questions remain open.

My background is in physics, and my work sits at the intersection of science, sustainability, space and communication. I am particularly interested in helping people engage with subjects that can seem inaccessible: physics, Earth observation, climate systems and space science.

## The work I do

### Learning resources and explanations

I create explanations, guides and learning materials that make technical subjects accessible without losing accuracy.

This can include introductory explanations, visual narratives, teaching resources, interactive examples and materials designed for specific age groups or audiences.

### Science communication

Science communication and education often overlap. Both involve making evidence meaningful, but education places greater emphasis on progression: helping someone move from an initial question to a deeper understanding.

I develop content that supports that progression, whether the subject is space, climate, physics, environmental data or digital technology.

### Educational technology

Digital tools can support learning when they are designed around a clear educational purpose. They can help people explore data, test ideas, visualise systems and see the consequences of different assumptions.

I build or shape lightweight digital resources that make information easier to explore, while keeping the underlying science and limitations visible.

### Public engagement

Public engagement is education outside the classroom. Talks, exhibitions, community projects, online resources and events can all help people encounter new ideas in ways that feel relevant to their lives.

I am interested in work that connects research to everyday experience, particularly around climate, space and the environment.
