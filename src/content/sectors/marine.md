# Marine

The marine environment covers oceans, seas, coasts and the systems that connect them. It includes marine science, ocean observation, conservation, maritime activity and the policies that shape how these systems are used and protected.

My work in this sector focuses on making ocean science and marine data easier to understand. I translate research, observations and environmental issues into clear communication that shows why ocean systems matter to people as well as to nature.

## Why it matters

Oceans regulate climate, support biodiversity, provide food and livelihoods, and influence weather and coastal resilience. They are also affected by warming, acidification, pollution, habitat loss and changing patterns of use.

Understanding the marine environment requires connecting physical processes, ecosystems and human activity. Ocean observations help reveal those connections and support monitoring, forecasting and environmental management.

## My perspective

I approach the marine environment as a dynamic system rather than a distant backdrop.

Ocean currents, temperature, salinity, sea level, marine life and human activity interact. Changes in one part of the system can affect others, which is why marine science needs both broad observation and careful interpretation.

My background is in physics, and my work sits at the intersection of marine science, climate, Earth observation and communication. I am particularly interested in how satellite and ocean data can make large-scale change more tangible and relevant.

## The work I do

### Ocean and coastal communication

I help explain marine science and ocean issues in ways that are accurate and engaging.

This can include ocean circulation, sea-level rise, coastal change, marine ecosystems, ocean observation and the relationship between oceans and climate.

### Making ocean data meaningful

Ocean data can reveal changes in temperature, sea level, currents, marine life and environmental conditions. But the value of that data depends on interpretation.

I help turn observations into clear narratives and visual explanations, showing what the data can tell us, what remains uncertain and why context matters.

### Earth observation and ocean monitoring

Satellites provide an important perspective on the ocean, observing sea-surface temperature, ocean colour, sea level, ice and other environmental indicators.

I am interested in how Earth observation can support marine monitoring, climate services and public understanding of ocean change.

### Conservation and sustainable use

Marine conservation involves protecting ecosystems while recognising the importance of oceans for food, transport, recreation and economic activity.

I help communicate these issues in ways that acknowledge both ecological limits and human dependence on marine systems.
