# Automotive

The automotive sector covers more than vehicles. It includes how they are designed, manufactured, powered, connected, used and eventually retired—and how they fit into wider mobility and energy systems.

My work in this sector focuses on making vehicle technology, electrification and low-emission mobility easier to understand. I translate technical and policy material into clear communication that supports informed decisions.

## Why it matters

Road transport affects emissions, air quality, energy demand, urban space and everyday mobility. The transition to lower-emission vehicles is therefore connected to climate goals, public health, energy systems and the design of towns and cities.

Electrification is an important part of this transition, but it is not the whole story. It also involves charging infrastructure, grid capacity, battery supply chains, vehicle efficiency, public transport, active travel and the way people use cars.

## My perspective

I approach automotive as part of a wider mobility and energy system.

A vehicle is not only a product. It is a node in a system involving energy generation, charging, roads, parking, freight, public transport and land use. Understanding those connections helps explain why decarbonising transport requires more than replacing one technology with another.

My background is in physics, and my work sits at the intersection of automotive, energy, climate and communication. I am particularly interested in making technical subjects accessible without losing the engineering and policy context.

## The work I do

### Explaining vehicle technology

I help explain how vehicles work and how their technology is changing.

This can include electric powertrains, batteries, charging, efficiency, safety systems, connectivity and the materials and manufacturing processes behind them.

### Communicating low-emission mobility

The transition to low-emission transport involves technology, infrastructure, behaviour and policy. Clear communication needs to distinguish between what is available today, what is developing and what remains uncertain.

I help organisations explain their role in that transition, including the evidence behind their claims and the practical constraints involved.

### Making data meaningful

Vehicle and mobility data can reveal patterns in usage, efficiency, charging, emissions and network demand.

I help turn this data into accessible narratives and visual explanations, connecting it to questions about cost, reliability, environmental impact and user experience.

### Supporting informed decisions

Whether the audience is a driver, fleet operator, policymaker or manufacturer, transport decisions involve trade-offs. Clear communication can help people understand those trade-offs and make better-informed choices.
