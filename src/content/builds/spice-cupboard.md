## What it does

Spice Cupboard & Mix Manager — keep track of the spices you have, and see which spice mixes you can make now or are one ingredient short of, with batch scaling.

## Features

- Keep track of the spices you have.
- Show mixes you can make now.
- Show mixes one ingredient short.
- Scale mixes by batch size.

## Who it is for

- Home cooks.

## Limitations

- Check quantities and allergens on your own ingredients.
