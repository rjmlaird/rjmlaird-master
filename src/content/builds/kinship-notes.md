## What it does

Kinship Notes — import a GEDCOM family tree, explore it as a whole tree, hourglass, pedigree, descendants or fan chart, and keep private research notes, questions and tasks against each person. Everything stays in your browser.

## Features

- Import a GEDCOM family tree.
- View as a whole tree, hourglass, pedigree, descendants or fan chart.
- Keep private notes, questions and tasks against each person.
- Keep everything in your browser.

## Who it is for

- Family historians.
- Genealogy hobbyists.

## Limitations

- Data stays in your browser, so clearing browser data may remove it.

## Status

This is in progress. Features and content may change.
