## What it does

Website Carbon Checker — estimate the carbon footprint of a web page from its transfer size, and check whether it is served from green hosting. A modelled estimate, not a measurement.

## Features

- Estimate a page's carbon footprint from its transfer size.
- Check whether it is served from green hosting.

## Who it is for

- Web developers.
- Designers.
- Content and accessibility teams.

## Limitations

- This is a modelled estimate, not a measurement.

## Status

This is planned and not yet available.
