## What it does

Julian Date & Sidereal Time — convert between UTC, Julian date, modified Julian date and Unix time, and calculate Greenwich and local mean sidereal time. Also available as a small JSON API.

## Features

- Convert between UTC, Julian date, modified Julian date and Unix time.
- Calculate Greenwich and local mean sidereal time.
- Access the results as a small JSON API.

## Who it is for

- Amateur astronomers.
- Astronomy and physics students.
- Educators.

## Limitations

- Results depend on the inputs and assumptions used; read them alongside the output.

## API access

The calculations are also available as a small JSON API.
