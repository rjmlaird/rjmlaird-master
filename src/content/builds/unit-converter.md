## What it does

Unit Converter — convert between physics, engineering and astronomy units, from newtons and pascals to parsecs, solar masses and electronvolts. Also available as a small JSON API.

## Features

- Convert physics, engineering and astronomy units.
- Cover units from newtons and pascals to parsecs, solar masses and electronvolts.
- Access conversions as a small JSON API.

## Who it is for

- Physics and engineering students.
- Educators.
- Curious learners.

## Limitations

- Results depend on the inputs and assumptions used; read them alongside the output.

## API access

The calculations are also available as a small JSON API.
