This site defines, documents and distributes the identity system used across the Ryan Laird digital estate. It is the source of truth for identity, voice, tokens, components and asset usage.

## What it is

- A brand guide and design-system reference.
- An asset-usage guide with approved previews and rules.
- A token documentation site, backed by a shared package.

## What it is not

- A portfolio, CV or marketing site.
- A photography gallery or digital asset manager.
- A client workspace or general wiki.

## How the systems divide the work

| System | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `rjmlaird.co.uk` | Professional home, services, selected work and contact |
| `brand.rjmlaird.co.uk` | How the ecosystem should look, sound and be implemented |
| `dam.rjmlaird.co.uk` | Asset records, masters, permissions and private source files |
| `cdn.rjmlaird.co.uk` | Public asset delivery |
| `portfolio.rjmlaird.co.uk` | Finished visual case studies |
| `images.rjmlaird.co.uk` | Public photography and visual archive |
| `labs.rjmlaird.co.uk` | Experiments and prototypes |
