## Principles

1. Clarity over cleverness
2. Evidence over hype
3. Usefulness over decoration
4. Curiosity without sensationalism
5. Technical confidence without unnecessary jargon
6. Accessibility from the beginning
7. Consistency with room for site-specific character

## Personality

Curious, precise, warm, evidence-led, practical, technically capable, editorial, calm and open to exploration.
