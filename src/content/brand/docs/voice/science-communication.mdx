---
title: Science communication
description: How to present science, data and claims responsibly.
order: 2
status: draft
---
- State the claim, then the evidence, then the limits.
- Link to the primary source where one exists.
- Explain a term the first time it appears, then use it consistently.
- Separate what is measured from what is inferred.
- Avoid superlatives such as "mind-blowing". Show why it matters instead.
- Say when something is a personal observation, not a result.
