The voice is curious, precise and warm. It explains rather than impresses.

<DoDont
  do={['Lead with what the reader needs', 'Use active voice', 'Name uncertainty when it exists', 'Keep sentences short enough to read aloud']}
  dont={['Use corporate jargon', 'Write overly casual social-media phrasing', 'Leave technical acronyms unexplained', 'Sound certain when the evidence is not']}
/>

## Tone by context

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Explainers and articles | Clear, curious, evidence-led |
| Services and booking | Direct, reassuring, specific |
| Errors and empty states | Plain, helpful, no apology |
| Labs and experiments | Playful, technical, honest about status |
