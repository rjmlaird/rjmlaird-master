## Stacks

- Sans: `system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, Roboto, sans-serif`
- Mono: `ui-monospace, Menlo, Consolas, monospace`

## Rules

- Body text is 1rem with a 1.6 line height and a measure of about 60 characters.
- Use sentence case for headings, buttons and labels.
- When a brand typeface is chosen and licensed, add it at the front of the stack and keep these fallbacks.
